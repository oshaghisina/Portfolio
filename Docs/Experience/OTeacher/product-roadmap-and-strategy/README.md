## Problem

OTeacher's own positioning analysis states the brand today is "one option among several" for
online language learning. The stated goal: become the "first choice," "a serious, influential
player" in the broader language-education market.

## My role

Interview-confirmed: Sina expanded beyond product/design execution into product strategy and
goal-setting after identifying that the redesign effort was blocked by broader product-leadership
and stakeholder-alignment problems. The diagnosis was that stakeholder needs were not reliably
aligned, and leadership lacked clarity about what to ask from each function to advance a desired
outcome.

This does not establish sole authorship. Sina worked in the Product unit — see the role-naming
discrepancy noted in [OTeacher/README.md](../README.md).

## Process

### Interview-confirmed alignment and strategy work

To address the alignment gap, the team solicited input from relevant stakeholders through
department-specific questionnaires. Participating groups were **Product**, **Marketing**,
**Education and teachers**, **Support/CRM**, **Engineering**, **Finance**, **Management**, and
**Operations**. The questions were designed to surface each department's needs, the value created
for learners, teachers, and the platform, and to clarify future direction and the scale of work
required.

The first stated output after this input-gathering was completion of the strategy file. The Figma
deck is relevant supporting material for that output, but does not capture all work performed.

### Figma-evidenced positioning and roadmap material

The positioning read feeds a three-horizon roadmap:

- **Fall 1402** — hosting/class platform, messenger, determining matchmaking level, specialized
  courses, group classes.
- **Winter 1402** — better tooling for teachers, new-market entry analysis, enterprise/corporate
  services, foreign-currency (FX) income analysis, a student roadmap.
- **Future** — a ticket manager, finance, a shop, offline courses, FX income (MVP), a new-market
  product (MVP).

Worth cross-checking: the "messenger" (پیام رسان) line item already has a matching screen in the
scanned Design file — see [panel-redesign](../panel-redesign/README.md)'s Notifications/Messenger
section — so it's further along than a bare roadmap line suggests. "Determine matchmaking level"
has no matching screen found (see [matchmaking-redesign](../matchmaking-redesign/README.md)).

In parallel, the deck documents a separate Education-unit program — see
