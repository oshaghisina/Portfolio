## Interview-confirmed scope and framing

The account below was confirmed in a live interview with Sina. It records legitimate scope that is
not necessarily visible in the Figma files; it should be kept distinct from claims evidenced by
the deck or Design file.

At entry, the intended product improvement/redesign exposed broader, deeper issues than interface
design alone. Sina diagnosed a product-leadership and alignment gap: stakeholder needs were not
being reliably aligned, and leadership did not have enough clarity about what to request from each
function in order to move a desired outcome forward.

Sina therefore expanded his contribution into product strategy and goal-setting. To address the
alignment gap, the team gathered input through department-specific questionnaires for **Product**,
**Marketing**, **Education and teachers**, **Support/CRM**, **Engineering**, **Finance**,
**Management**, and **Operations**. The questions were intended to surface each department's
needs, the value it could create for learners, teachers, and the platform, and to clarify future
direction and the scale of work required. The first stated output after this input-gathering was
completion of the strategy file.

The [Product Strategy & Roadmap](product-roadmap-and-strategy/README.md) document is the primary
record for this work. Its Figma deck supplies supporting material, but is not a complete record of
the interview-confirmed leadership, alignment, strategy, and goal-setting work.
