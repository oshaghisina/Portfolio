# Glossary

Terms used across the docs and, later, on the site. Each term is expanded once per page on the
site and linked here in the docs. Plain meanings are written for a reader outside the field.

## Business & marketing

| Term | Expansion | Plain meaning | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| NMV | Net merchandise value | What customers actually paid after discounts, returns and cancellations | Digikala |
| GMV | Gross merchandise value | Total value of everything sold, before discounts and returns | comparison with NMV |
| CTR | Click-through rate | Of the people who saw it, how many clicked | Digikala dashboards |
| CPC | Cost per click | What each click cost in ad spend | Digikala dashboards |
| CPA | Cost per acquisition | What each new customer cost to acquire | performance marketing |
| CR / CVR | Conversion rate | Of the people who arrived, how many did the thing (bought, signed up) | Digikala, Carsparency |
| ROAS | Return on ad spend | Revenue earned per unit of ad spend | performance marketing |
| CAC | Customer acquisition cost | Total cost of winning one customer, all channels | growth |
| LTV | Lifetime value | What a customer is worth over their whole relationship | growth |
| NPS | Net promoter score | "Would you recommend us?" scored −100 to +100 | Arvan Cloud |
| A/B/n testing | — | Showing different versions to different users and measuring which works | Skills |
| BNPL | Buy now, pay later | Paying in installments, usually interest-free, at checkout | Digikala concept |
| Gold-backed credit | — | Borrowing against gold you already hold as collateral | Digikala concept |
| Zero-fee campaign | — | A period where trading fees are waived to attract users | Digikala |
| GTM | Go-to-market (strategy) | The plan for launching something: who, through which channels, with what message | Skills |
| KPI | Key performance indicator | The handful of numbers a team steers by | dashboards |
| MAU / DAU | Monthly / daily active users | People who used the product at least once in the period | growth |
| Segmentation | — | Grouping users by traits or behaviour so each group gets relevant messaging | Digikala |
| Event-driven engagement | — | Messages triggered by what a user just did (or stopped doing) rather than on a schedule | Digikala automation |
| Performance marketing | — | Paid channels judged on measurable results (clicks, sign-ups, sales) | Digikala |
| BI | Business intelligence | Turning raw data into dashboards and reports a team can steer by | Digikala ("BI developer", BI dashboards) |
| B2C | Business to consumer | Products sold to individuals rather than to companies | A1Paradise |
| GA4 | Google Analytics 4 | Google's current web/app analytics product | Tools |
| PR | Public relations | Earned coverage — press and influencers — as opposed to paid ads | Digikala, Hadish Mall |

## Design & product

| Term | Expansion | Plain meaning | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|---|
| IA | Information architecture | How content is organised, labelled and navigated | Arvan Cloud, Skills |
| IDF | Interaction Design Foundation | Online design school; Sina takes its courses | Education |
| Lean UX | — | Designing in short build–measure–learn loops instead of big upfront specs | Didestan |
| Design sprint | — | A time-boxed (usually 5-day) process to prototype and test an idea | OTeacher |
| Mid-fidelity (mid-fi) | — | Prototypes with real layout and flow but placeholder visuals | Didestan |
| Material (Design) | Google Material Design | Google's design system and component library | Didestan |
| Design system | — | A shared library of components, tokens and rules so teams build consistently and fast | Biomaze |
| Usability testing | — | Watching real people try to complete tasks and noting where they struggle | Carsparency |
| Prototype | — | A clickable mock-up used to test an idea before building it | several |
| Roadmap | — | The sequenced plan of what a product team will build and why | OTeacher |
| Matchmaking | — | Pairing two sides of a marketplace (here: teachers and students) | OTeacher |

## Repo & CMS terms

| Term | Plain meaning | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Frontmatter | The YAML block at the top of a Markdown file holding structured fields | every doc |
| Collection | A content type in Payload (e.g. `projects`); each entry is a document | Content-Model |
| Global | A single-instance content type in Payload (e.g. header, footer, about) | Content-Model |
| Block | A reusable content section an editor can add to a page layout | Content-Model |
| Hero | The top section of a page | Payload template |
| Slug | The URL-safe identifier for a page or file (`arvan-cloud`) | everywhere |
| Locale | One language version of the content (`en`, `fa`) | D-009 |
| RTL | Right-to-left — the writing direction of Persian | Design benchmarks |
| Seed | Loading initial content into the CMS programmatically | Content-Model |
| Machine-owned | A block regenerated by a script; don't edit it by hand | README |
