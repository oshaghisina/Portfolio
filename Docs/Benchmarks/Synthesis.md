# Benchmark Synthesis

What the benchmarks say *together*. Individual files in [Content/](Content/) and [Design/](Design/)
describe one site each; this page turns them into patterns and, from patterns, into decisions
for Sina's site. Anything that changes the site's shape is mirrored in
[../Content-Model.md](../Content-Model.md) and, if it's a firm decision, in
[../Decisions.md](../Decisions.md).

**Update rule:** when the number of benchmark files exceeds `benchmarks_reviewed` by 3 or more,
update this page (`docs:validate` warns). Record the update in the log at the bottom.

## How to read this page

- **Pattern** — something seen in ≥ 2 benchmarks, named plainly.
- **Verdict** — `will` (we adopt), `won't` (we deliberately don't), `maybe` (needs more evidence or
  a design decision first).
- Everything here is provisional until it lands in Decisions.md.
